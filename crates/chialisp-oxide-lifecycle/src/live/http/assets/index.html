What this view establishes

Costs are local, height-specific preflight measurements: execution, conditions, and charged plain generator bytes. They are not whole-block costs or network fee quotes. A saved confirmation records provider-observed exact spend bytes and expected child coins; it is not authenticated bundle inclusion or finality.

Inputs marked unspent belong to that transfer. Signed bundles, hashes, outputs and observations are public. No private-key or recovery-phrase entry belongs in this page.
