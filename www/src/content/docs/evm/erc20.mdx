## Balances are coins, and the contract is two puzzles

An ERC-20 contract keeps one `balanceOf` mapping and moves numbers between
entries. A Chia CAT (Chia Asset Token) has no balance table. Each holding is a
separate coin whose puzzle wraps the owner’s inner puzzle in the standard CAT
outer layer, and the CAT layer makes every spend conserve the token’s total
across the coins it touches.

What the ERC-20 constructor and `mint` decide (who may create supply) is the
job of a **TAIL** (Token and Asset Issuance Limitations) program. This export is
the canonical genesis-by-coin-ID TAIL ported to Rust: it allows issuance only in
a spend of one specific parent coin, so supply is fixed at creation. The
[CAT TAIL tutorial](/chialisp-oxide/tutorials/cat-genesis-tail/) walks through a
full local issuance and transfer.

Some ERC-20 ideas have no counterpart: there is no `approve`/`transferFrom`
allowance, because an owner signs each spend of their own coins, and nothing
calls into the token contract.
