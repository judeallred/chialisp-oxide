## Chia: the closest corpus pattern

There is no auction export in the corpus yet. The nearest pattern is the
[hot-potato game](/chialisp-oxide/tutorials/spuds/): one self-recreating coin
holds the pot, each new participant adds funds and becomes the holder by signing
the successor, a relative-height window restarts on every take, and the last
holder claims once the window passes. That matches the auction’s “highest bidder
when time runs out” shape.

It is not an auction port, and the differences matter:

- **No refunds.** The contract records outbid amounts in `bids` and lets bidders
  `withdraw`. Spuds keeps every contribution in the pot. A Chia auction would
  have to pay the previous high bidder back inside the same spend that accepts a
  higher bid, so there is no withdrawal step to forget.
- **Fixed increments.** Spuds takes exactly 1,000 mojos per round. An auction
  needs bids of any size above the current one, with the bid amount checked by
  `ASSERT_MY_AMOUNT` on the recreated coin.
- **Sealed bids differ again.** A blind auction hides bids until a reveal phase,
  which is the [commit-wait-reveal](/chialisp-oxide/tutorials/precommit/)
  pattern.

Treat this page as a design sketch, not a verified port.
