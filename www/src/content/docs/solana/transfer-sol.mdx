## A signer check becomes a signature condition

The Solana processor moves lamports between accounts, either through the System
Program (which checks that the payer signed) or by adjusting balances of
accounts the program owns.

A Chia coin cannot be partially debited. Spending it destroys the whole coin,
and its puzzle decides what new coins exist afterwards. This export returns
`AGG_SIG_ME(OWNER, message)` together with a payment to a fixed recipient.
Consensus accepts the spend only if the transaction carries the owner’s
signature over that message, bound to this coin and network; without it, the
spend is rejected.

Any value the conditions don’t pay out becomes a transaction fee. That is why
puzzles assert their own amount and create change coins explicitly. See
[split a payment](/chialisp-oxide/tutorials/split-payment/).
