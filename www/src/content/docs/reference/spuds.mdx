**WIP prototype. Not ready for use or real funds.** Spuds is an original teaching
policy. One pot coin commits its selected module, holder key, payout hash and
round. A take adds exactly 1,000 mojos; a claim pays the current holder. Counts
stop at round eight. It is not a singleton or CAT.

The relative timer belongs to each actual pot coin. Takes require age at least
one and below five; claims require age at least five. A take creates a new coin
and resets the timer. The new holder signs an intent committing the exact
successor, while the top-up asserts that take's announcement. Key widths alone
establish neither valid BLS keys nor destination recoverability.

The simulator's quoted top-up is a composition fixture. Spuds has no standard
wallet integration, funding workflow or live-network lane. These results do not
claim liveness, inclusion, finality or compiler preservation.
